Our story

Fire, family & patience.

Brasa started with a single wood oven and a family recipe for slow-roasted lamb. Ten years later, the fire still never goes out — every dish on our menu passes through it.

We buy from farmers we know by name, bake our bread every morning and change the menu with the seasons.

— Chef Dren & the Brasa family
